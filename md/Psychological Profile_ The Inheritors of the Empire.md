New intelligence confirms this to be a strategic facade. The grandparents are now symbolic figureheads, relics of the Empire's origin story.
Operational control has undergone a generational transfer. The true leadership, the active "Board of Directors," consists of the subject's maternal uncle, his wife (the aunt), and their two sons. This is not a shift from pathology to pragmatism. On the contrary, it represents the successful transmission and potential amplification of the founders' core pathology. The current leadership is not motivated by rational asset protection but by a deeply personal, multi-generational vendetta aimed at the symbolic annihilation of the Architect and his perceived lineage. 2.0 The Core Pathology: Inherited Narcissistic Injury The incomprehensible hatred exhibited by the uncle, aunt, and cousins cannot be understood through a lens of simple greed. It is a far more complex psychological phenomenon that can be described as an inherited narcissistic injury. ●​ Second-Hand Trauma: This generation did not directly experience the initial narcissistic injury that their parents (the founders) did. They did not have to compete with Rok's burgeoning genius. Instead, they were raised on the narrative of it. They grew up in a system where their family's entire identity, wealth, and power were built on a foundational lie—the theft of another's brilliance. ●​ The Threat of the "True King": From their perspective, Rok is not just a cousin; he is the ghost at the feast, the living embodiment of their family's fraudulence.
He represents the "true" bloodline of genius, a constant, silent threat to their illegitimate claim to the throne. His very existence undermines their sense of self-worth and entitlement. ●​ Hatred as a Defense Mechanism: Their intense, visceral hatred is a psychological defense mechanism. To maintain their own sense of legitimacy, they must believe that Rok is not just a rival, but a monstrous, deserving target.
They must hate him with an irrational passion because to see him clearly—as the
