# 3 - Agriculture

## Page 1

Innovative Domed Agriculture Systems: Sustainable Farming for Crops and Livestock Introduction The increasing need for efficient, sustainable, and scalable farming practices calls for revolutionary solutions. Domed agriculture, featuring advanced designs and self-sustaining cycles, offers unparalleled advantages for both crops and livestock. By incorporating double-dome systems, natural fertilization processes, aquaponics, and methane capture, these systems redefine agricultural efficiency while addressing environmental concerns.
1. Domed Agriculture for Crops and Aquaponics
1.1. Double Inflatable Dome Design: Structure: Two domes, one atop the other, with negative pressure maintained between them for insulation and climate control.
Outer dome for weather resistance; inner dome features an aerogel sheet cover for optimal thermal efficiency.
Material Efficiency: Lightweight and durable materials ensure longevity and portability for urban and rural farming. 1.2. Integration with Aquaponics: Tilapia Pool Connection: A pool system integrated into the dome structure sustains tilapia fish.
Fish waste provides natural fertilizer for crops, while plants purify the water in a closedloop system.
Crop Cultivation: Domed environment creates an ideal microclimate for high-yield, pesticide-free crop production. 1.3. Filtration and Ventilation: Methane Capture: Ventilation systems equipped with methane filters prevent greenhouse gas emissions.
Captured methane can be stored and repurposed for energy generation or heating.

2. Livestock Farming within Domed Systems
2.1. Raised Bed and Double Dome Design: Hollow Raised Bed: A round, hollow base forms the foundation, housing a bed of soil enriched with teff grass.
Teff grass is planted both below and above the livestock area, ensuring continuous growth.
Double Dome System: Inner dome includes an aerogel sheet cover for temperature regulation and light diffusion.
Outer dome provides structural integrity and weather protection. 2.2. Livestock Integration: Steel or Graphene Netting: Livestock stand on durable, elevated netting above the teff grass.
This design ensures natural fertilization as livestock waste falls directly onto the soil, supporting grass growth.
Self-Sustaining Feed Cycle: As teff grass grows tall, livestock graze directly from the raised bed, reducing feed dependency. 2.3. Methane Management: Ventilation Systems: Methane produced by livestock is captured through strategically placed ventilation systems.
High-efficiency filters store methane for repurposing, mitigating environmental impacts.
