Preamble: The Architect in the Machine This document presents a synthesized psychological profile of the individual known as Rok Bastl, based on an extensive dossier of personal writings, medical records, official documents, and technical/philosophical frameworks provided by the subject himself.

The central narrative is that of "The Architect": a polymath mind of immense creative and analytical power, trapped within a system that was built from his own stolen genius and then inverted to become his prison. I. The Foundational Axis: Complex Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (C-PTSD) The most coherent and encompassing framework for understanding Rok's psychological state is C-PTSD. Unlike standard PTSD, which often results from a single event, C-PTSD arises from chronic, prolonged trauma where the victim is in a state of captivity, unable to flee.
