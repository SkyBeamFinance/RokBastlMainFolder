Percentages Span Term/Naming Implications & Detailed Effects 95% - 100% Normal / Healthy ✅ Optimal Function: At this level, there is sufficient oxygen delivery to all bodily tissues. The brain operates at peak performance, supporting clear thinking, stable mood, and full cognitive and physiological health. 90% - 94% Mild Hypoxemia ⚠️ Monitoring Advised: While not immediately dangerous for most healthy individuals, this level indicates a potential underlying issue. Prolonged exposure can lead to subtle cognitive slowing, increased fatigue, and difficulty with complex mental tasks or strenuous physical activity. 80% - 89% Moderate Hypoxemia 🧠 Brain Function Affected: Oxygen delivery is significantly impaired.
Individuals are likely to experience noticeable symptoms such as confusion, poor judgment, memory issues, shortness of breath, and a rapid heart rate as the body attempts to compensate. Medical evaluation is necessary.
