# Materials and Computing Documents

This file serves as an index of significant documents related to materials and computing technologies that illustrate various technological paradigm shifts.

1. **A First-Principles Critique of Silicon Dominance.pdf**  
   This document presents an in-depth analysis of silicon's long-standing dominance in the computing industry, exploring alternative materials and their potential advantages in future technologies.

2. **Beyond Silicon: A Practical Blueprint For Bismuth (Bi₂Te₃)‑class Computation (1).pdf**  
   This paper proposes a roadmap for the adoption of bismuth-based materials in computing, discussing their unique properties and potential applications in the next generation of devices.

3. **8 - Materials Science and Structural Engineering.pdf**  
   An exploration of advancements in materials science that directly impact structural engineering, emphasizing materials that enable innovative architectural designs and constructions.

4. **Oem-odm Laptop Blueprint.pdf**  
   This blueprint outlines a reference design for OEM and ODM laptop manufacturers, highlighting key materials and computing considerations for optimizing performance and sustainability.
